Case Study · Project Coordination

Two teams, one workspace, zero missed deadlines.

A Monday.com workspace built to give a sales and marketing campaign full visibility into who owned what, and when it was due.

Client
Sales & Marketing Team
Tools
Monday.com
Role
Project Coordination
Result
0 missed deadlines

The problem

A sales and marketing campaign was running across two teams with no shared view of task status, due dates, or who owned what — deadlines were being tracked in separate notes and memory instead of one system.

What I built

I built a Monday.com workspace tracking task status, due dates, priorities, and budget in one place, grouped into To-Do, Doing, and Completed views so anyone could see the state of the campaign at a glance.

Marketing campaign deployment plan — subitems, owners, priority, and budget tracked per task.

Tasks grouped by status — To-Do, Doing, Completed — with budget and timeline visible in the same view.

I also built a lightweight daily operations tracker for the team's day-to-day workload, so recurring tasks didn't need their own separate system.

Result

Outcome

Both teams launched on schedule with zero missed deadlines and full visibility into task ownership.
