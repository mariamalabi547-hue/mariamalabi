Case Study · Customer Support

Support tickets that stopped living in someone's inbox.

Setting up Freshdesk and Zendesk so customer questions had a queue, an owner, and a status — instead of a shared inbox nobody fully trusted.

Client
Customer-facing Business
Tools
Freshdesk, Zendesk
Role
Support Desk Setup
Result
One ticket queue

The problem

Customer questions were coming in through email, DMs, and web forms with no single place to see what was open, what was answered, and what had gone quiet. Nothing had an owner until someone happened to notice it.

What I built

I set up Freshdesk as the primary support desk — every inbound question becomes a ticket with a status, priority, and assigned owner, so nothing sits unanswered without someone noticing.

Every inbound question logged as a ticket — status, priority, and owner visible at a glance.

For a second client already using Zendesk, I configured the queue and views the same way, so the same discipline applied regardless of which platform the business had already committed to.

Result

Outcome

Every customer question now lands in one queue with a clear owner and status — nothing depends on someone remembering to check an inbox.
