Case Study · Content Operations

Five contributors, three languages, zero overlap.

Two content coordination builds — a wellness brand's challenge launch, and a multilingual campaign board running a five-person content team.

Clients
Wellness Brand, Campaign Team
Tools
Trello, Task Boards
Role
Content Ops Coordination
Result
Zero task overlap

Project 1 — Wellness content launch

A wellness brand needed to launch its "Mindful Living Challenge" with content and deadlines tracked across a small team. I built a Trello board with content ideas, goals, checklists, and due dates assigned per task, so every piece of content had a clear owner from idea to publish.

Content ideas move left to right — from idea, to draft, to client review, to scheduled and published.

Project 2 — Multilingual campaign coordination

A separate, higher-stakes campaign needed to bring order to a high-volume, time-sensitive rollout where multiple content creators, caption writers, and a scheduler were all working at once. Each day was mapped to a specific content theme, with tasks, deadlines, priority levels, and platform targets tracked centrally — across English, French, and Arabic.

Day-by-day task board — every post tracked by language, platform, priority, and status.

Result

Outcome

A team of 5+ contributors executed a coordinated, multilingual content campaign with clear ownership, zero task overlap, and on-time publishing across all platforms.
