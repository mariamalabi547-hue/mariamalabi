Case Study · Automation

Leads sorted by value, before a human ever sees them.

A Zapier automation that routes every new lead into a budget tier, notifies the team instantly, and drops it straight into the sales pipeline.

Client
Sales-led Business
Tools
Zapier, Google Forms, Slack
Role
Lead Routing Automation
Result
Instant tiered routing

The problem

Every new lead came in through the same form, regardless of budget — a $200/month inquiry and a $5,000/month inquiry landed in the same inbox, got the same priority, and waited for the same person to notice and triage them manually.

What I built

I built a Google Form to capture new leads, including their budget range, then automated everything after submission with Zapier.

The intake form — budget range is captured up front, which drives everything downstream.

Based on the budget field, the Zap routes each lead down one of three paths automatically:

Path A — leads above $2,000/month get flagged as high-priority.

Path B — the $500–$2,000 range, routed with its own follow-up sequence.

Path C — under $500, still followed up automatically, just with a lighter-touch sequence.

Each path triggers a Slack alert to the team the moment a lead lands, and logs the lead into a tracked sales pipeline so nothing gets followed up twice or missed entirely.

Every lead lands in the pipeline already sorted by tier, with the team notified in real time.

Result

Outcome

High-value leads get noticed and followed up on first, automatically — no manual triage, no leads slipping through, and the whole team knows the moment a new one lands.
