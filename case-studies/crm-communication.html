Case Study · CRM & Inbox

From a buried inbox to zero missed messages.

Rule-based email filters and a full CRM migration, built for a client whose client relationships were slipping through the cracks.

Client
Service-based business
Tools
Gmail Filters, HubSpot
Role
Inbox & CRM Rebuild
Result
0 missed emails

The problem

The client's inbox was disorganized, causing slow follow-ups and missed messages. Client contact details lived in scattered notes, not one system anyone could trust.

What I built

I built filtered, rule-based email folders to sort and prioritize incoming messages automatically, so nothing urgent could sit unread in a crowded inbox.

Filtered inbox system — incoming mail is sorted and prioritized before it's ever manually opened.

I then migrated contact tracking into HubSpot CRM, giving the client one place to see every customer's history instead of hunting across email threads.

Centralized customer records — name, contact info, and status in one searchable table.

Result

Outcome

Response time improved, missed-email incidents dropped to zero, and all client records are now centralized and current.
