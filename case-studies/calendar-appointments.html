Case Study · Scheduling

A calendar that stopped double-booking itself.

Color-coded time-blocking and a self-serve booking page for a coaching client running back-to-back sessions.

Client
1:1 Coaching Practice
Tools
Google Calendar, Calendly
Role
Calendar & Scheduling Build
Result
Zero conflicts

The problem

A coaching client needed a reliable system to manage back-to-back sessions, admin tasks, and personal time — without double-booking clients or losing personal time to work that snuck onto the calendar.

What I built

A color-coded Google Calendar with time-blocking for meetings, tasks, and deadlines, so every category of time had its own lane and nothing could silently overlap.

The client-facing appointment page — clients pick a slot directly from the coach's real availability.

On top of that, I set up a Calendly page with limited weekly consultation slots for both 1:1 and group sessions, so bookings never had to go through a back-and-forth email thread.

Booking flow: client picks a date, a time, and confirms — no messages exchanged.

Result

Outcome

Scheduling conflicts dropped to zero, and manual back-and-forth booking emails were eliminated entirely.
