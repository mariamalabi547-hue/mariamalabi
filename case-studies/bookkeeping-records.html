Case Study · Bookkeeping

Books that balance without a monthly scramble.

A structured stock and financial-records system built so month-end never turns into a week-long reconciliation project.

Client
Retail / Inventory Business
Tools
Excel, QuickBooks Online
Role
Bookkeeping Systems
Result
Audit-ready books

The problem

Stock counts, product costs, and incoming purchase data lived in separate files that were rarely updated at the same time — so the "official" numbers rarely matched what was actually on the shelf.

What I built

I built a stock balance sheet that reconciles opening stock, purchases, and sales into one live closing figure, so stock levels stay trustworthy without a manual recount.

Stock balance sheet — opening stock, purchases, and sales roll up into a live closing balance.

Alongside it, a product master sheet standardized how every item was named, priced, and categorized, so the same product couldn't quietly exist under three different names across different reports.

A dedicated input sheet gave the team one place to log new purchases and transactions, feeding directly into the balance sheet instead of being re-typed across multiple files.

Result

Outcome

One reconciled, audit-ready record of stock and spending — closing the books became a five-minute check instead of a week of cross-referencing.
