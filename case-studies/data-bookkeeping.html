Case Study · Data & Bookkeeping

One source of truth, instead of a dozen scattered files.

Structured tracking systems for registrations, payments, and inventory — built so nothing depends on anyone's memory.

Client
Training Program & Retail Business
Tools
Excel, Apollo.io, QuickBooks
Role
Data & Records Systems
Result
1 source of truth

The problem

A training program needed a structured way to track registrations and payments across dozens of participants — the information existed, but it wasn't trustworthy or centralized.

What I built

I built an Excel system to log registration dates, payment status, and course details for each entrant, so the team could see at a glance who had paid and who hadn't.

I also used Apollo.io to source and organize leads by industry and company size, turning a vague target market into a focused, ready-to-contact list.

Records tracking system — every entry logged with clear status and ownership.

For a related retail client, I built out sales and inventory tracking on the same principle: one trustworthy source instead of several conflicting spreadsheets.

Result

Outcome

Created one accurate source of truth for registrants and a focused, ready-to-contact lead list by target industry.
